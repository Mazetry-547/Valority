Values of the Game

At Valority, we believe that teaching values is the most important lesson we can give our children. These are the core values we instill through our game:

Empathy

The ability to understand and share the feelings of others. It means imagining how someone else feels and offering support when needed.

Respect

Treating others with kindness and consideration, even when they have different opinions or beliefs. It means listening, being polite and respectful.

Honesty

Always telling the truth and acting with sincerity. It involves being trustworthy, not lying or cheating, and choosing to do what is right.

Our Story

Can you imagine teaching kids values while they have fun and learn through play? That’s the question we asked ourselves a year ago, and that’s how Valority was born. Valority is an app we created during a Microsoft hackathon to help fix the lack of education on emotions and values in schools. We saw that many children grow up without learning important values like empathy, respect, and honesty. So, we looked for a fun, creative, and interactive way to help them learn these values from a young age. With mini-games, stories, and challenges, Valority teaches kids simple lessons they can use in everyday life. In 2025, with new team members and the project growing into a company, our mission is still the same. We want to help children grow emotionally and learn to be kind, responsible, and caring people who make the world better. Join us and build your values!
